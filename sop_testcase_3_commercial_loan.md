# Commercial Loan Underwriting & Multi-Tier Risk Assessment

## Document Metadata & Scope
- **Purpose:** This SOP details the process for underwriting, risk assessment, committee approval, and post-disbursement monitoring of commercial loans ranging from $100,000 to $10,000,000. It outlines the roles of various teams, the procedural steps from origination to disbursement, key decision points, exception handling, and service level agreements for timely processing.
- **Target Audience:** Underwriters, Risk Assessors, Credit Committee Members, Loan Officers
- **Auditable Verification:** PASS (100% Entailed, 0 Unsupported Claims, 100% Rule Coverage)

## 1. Purpose

1. Purpose [S002] This SOP governs the end-to-end underwriting, risk assessment, committee approval, and post-disbursement monitoring process for commercial loans ranging from $100,000 to $10,000,000, involving multiple parallel workstreams (Credit, Legal, Collateral Valuation, Compliance) that converge at a final Credit Committee decision gate [S003].

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all commercial and corporate lending applications originated through the Corporate Banking division [S005]. It governs the end-to-end underwriting, risk assessment, committee approval, and post-disbursement monitoring process for commercial loans [S003]. Specifically, the loan amounts covered range from $100,000 to $10,000,000 [S003]. This procedure is designed for deals involving multiple parallel workstreams, including Credit, Legal, Collateral Valuation, and Compliance, which all converge for a final Credit Committee decision [S003]. Syndicated loans are explicitly excluded from this SOP, as they are covered under a separate procedure [S005].

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

This section outlines the distinct roles and responsibilities of the various teams and individuals involved in the commercial loan underwriting and approval process.

The **Relationship Manager (RM)** originates the deal and liaises with the client [S007]. The RM's responsibilities include originating the opportunity and submitting a Deal Screening Form along with preliminary financial statements (last 3 years audited) and a business plan [S015].

The **Underwriter** performs financial analysis and prepares the Credit Memo [S008]. Key procedural steps involve beginning detailed financial spreading and ratio analysis [S018], building a 5-year financial model, calculating key ratios (Debt Service Coverage Ratio, Leverage Ratio, Current Ratio), and benchmarking against industry norms [S019]. The Underwriter assigns an internal Risk Rating (1-10 scale) using the Bank's proprietary credit scoring model, incorporating financial ratios, management quality, and industry outlook factors [S020]. Upon completion of parallel workstreams, the Underwriter consolidates findings into a formal Credit Memo, including Risk Rating, LTV, proposed covenants, pricing, and a recommendation [S025]. If any material adverse change in the borrower's financial position is identified after Credit Memo submission but before disbursement, the Underwriter must refresh the analysis and re-submit to the original approval authority before funds are released [S058].

The **Collateral Valuation Team** independently appraises pledged collateral [S010]. This team schedules an independent site visit and appraisal [S018] and completes the appraisal, submitting a Valuation Report with the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio [S021].

The **Compliance Officer** conducts AML/sanctions screening and regulatory checks [S011]. The Compliance Officer initiates AML, sanctions, and adverse media screening on the borrower and its beneficial owners [S018] and completes the screening process [S023].

**Legal Counsel** reviews loan documentation and the covenant structure [S009]. This role includes reviewing the proposed covenant package and drafting or reviewing the Loan Agreement, Security Documents, and Guarantee (if applicable) [S026]. Legal Counsel is also responsible for finalizing all documentation and coordinating execution/signing with the client [S031].

The **Credit Committee** is a cross-functional body that provides final approval for loans above $1,000,000, with delegated authority applying below this threshold [S012]. The Credit Committee reviews the Deal Package for loans where the loan amount is $1,000,000 or above, OR the Risk Rating is 6-10 regardless of amount [S029]. The committee reviews the package, may request additional information (looping back to the Underwriter), and votes to Approve, Approve with Modified Conditions, or Decline [S030].

The **Loan Servicing Team** manages disbursement, covenant monitoring, and reporting post-approval [S013]. Their process includes processing the initial disbursement upon full execution of documents and satisfaction of all Conditions Precedent [S032]. The team also sets up ongoing covenant monitoring, such as financial covenant testing quarterly and collateral revaluation annually, and schedules the first compliance certificate due date [S033].

The **Financial Crime Unit** receives escalated deals if a confirmed sanctions/AML hit occurs [S024].

**Senior Risk Management** may be escalated to for a policy exception if the appraised LTV exceeds the Bank's policy maximum [S022].

The **Chief Risk Officer** provides additional approval for any deal exceeding the Bank's Single Borrower Limit or Industry Concentration Limit, regardless of Risk Rating [S056].

## 4. Process Steps

The Relationship Manager (RM) originates the loan opportunity [S015] and submits a Deal Screening Form along with preliminary financial statements (last 3 years audited) and a business plan [S015]. An Underwriter performs an initial screening to confirm the deal aligns with the Bank's target market and risk appetite, considering industry, geography, and deal size [S016]. If a deal fails the initial screening, the RM is notified with the reasons, and the deal is archived as 'Declined - Pre-Screening' [S017].

If the deal passes screening, three parallel workstreams are initiated simultaneously [S018]:
1.  **Underwriter:** Performs detailed financial spreading and ratio analysis [S018]. The Underwriter builds a 5-year financial model [S019], calculates key ratios including Debt Service Coverage Ratio, Leverage Ratio, and Current Ratio [S019], and benchmarks these against industry norms [S019]. The Underwriter then assigns an internal Risk Rating on a 1-10 scale using the Bank's proprietary credit scoring model, incorporating financial ratios, management quality, and industry outlook factors [S020].
2.  **Collateral Valuation Team:** Schedules an independent site visit and appraisal [S018]. The team completes the appraisal and submits a Valuation Report that includes the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio [S021]. If the appraised LTV exceeds the Bank's policy maximum (typically 75%), the deal is flagged for either additional collateral, a reduced loan amount, or escalation to Senior Risk Management for a policy exception [S022]. If the appraised LTV is within policy, processing proceeds [S041].
3.  **Compliance Officer:** Initiates AML, sanctions, and adverse media screening on the borrower and its beneficial owners [S018]. The Officer completes screening [S023]. If a confirmed sanctions or AML hit occurs (not a false positive), the deal is immediately escalated to the Financial Crime Unit, and processing is suspended pending investigation [S024]. If compliance screening is clear or a false positive, processing proceeds [S044].

Once all three workstreams are complete and cleared, the Underwriter consolidates findings into a formal Credit Memo [S025]. The Credit Memo includes the Risk Rating, LTV, proposed covenants, pricing, and a recommendation (Approve / Approve with Conditions / Decline) [S025]. Legal Counsel reviews the proposed covenant package [S026] and drafts or reviews the Loan Agreement, Security Documents, and Guarantee (if applicable) [S026]. The Credit Memo, Valuation Report, Compliance Clearance, and draft Legal Documents are compiled into a single Deal Package for committee review [S027].

**Approval Routing:**
- If the loan amount is below $1,000,000 and the Risk Rating is 1-5 (lower risk), the deal can be approved via Delegated Credit Authority by two authorized signatories, without requiring full committee review [S028].
- If the loan amount is $1,000,000 or above, OR the Risk Rating is 6-10 (higher risk) regardless of amount, the Deal Package is submitted to the Credit Committee for review at their next scheduled session [S029].

**Credit Committee Review:**
The Credit Committee reviews the Deal Package [S030], may request additional information from the Underwriter [S030], and votes to Approve, Approve with Modified Conditions, or Decline the loan [S030].
- If the Credit Committee approves the loan, processing proceeds to documentation [S049].
- If the Credit Committee approves with modified conditions, the Underwriter updates the Credit Memo and terms before documentation [S050].
- If the Credit Committee declines the loan, the RM notifies the client, and the deal is archived [S051].

**Documentation and Disbursement:**
If Approved (either via Delegated Authority or Committee), Legal Counsel finalizes all loan documentation [S031] and coordinates the execution and signing of loan documents with the client [S031]. Upon full execution of documents and satisfaction of all Conditions Precedent (e.g., insurance certificates, lien registration confirmation), the Loan Servicing Team processes the initial disbursement [S032]. Disbursement may be processed in tranches for construction or project loans [S032].

**Post-Disbursement Monitoring:**
The Loan Servicing Team sets up ongoing covenant monitoring [S033], which includes financial covenant testing quarterly [S033] and collateral revaluation annually [S033]. The Loan Servicing Team also schedules the first compliance certificate due date [S033].
- If a covenant breach is detected, a Waiver Request/Remediation Plan workflow is initiated, which may loop back to the Credit Committee [S053].
- If no covenant breach is detected, the standard monitoring cycle continues [S054].

**Pre-Disbursement Review for Adverse Changes:**
Any material adverse change in the borrower's financial position identified after Credit Memo submission but before disbursement requires the Underwriter to refresh the analysis and re-submit to the original approval authority before funds are released [S058].

## 5. Decision Points

Decision 1: Does the deal pass initial screening? [S036] If no, the deal is declined and archived as 'Declined - Pre-Screening'. [S037] If yes, proceed to parallel workstreams. [S038]

Decision 2: Is the appraised LTV within policy? [S039] If the appraised LTV exceeds the Bank's policy maximum, additional collateral is required, the loan amount must be reduced, or the case must be escalated for a policy exception. [S040] If the appraised LTV is within policy, proceed. [S041]

Decision 3: Compliance screening result. [S042] If a confirmed hit is identified (not a false positive), escalate to the Financial Crime Unit and suspend processing. [S043] If the screening is clear or a false positive, proceed. [S044]

Decision 4: Approval routing. [S045] If the loan amount is less than $1,000,000 AND the Risk Rating is between 1-5, approval is routed through Delegated Credit Authority. [S046] If the loan amount is $1,000,000 or greater OR the Risk Rating is between 6-10, the Deal Package is submitted to the Credit Committee for review. [S047]

Decision 5: Credit Committee vote. [S048] If the vote is to Approve, proceed to documentation. [S049] If Approve with Modified Conditions, the Underwriter updates the Credit Memo and terms before documentation. [S050] If Decline, the RM notifies the client, and the deal is archived. [S051]

Decision 6: Covenant monitoring result. [S052] If a breach is detected, initiate the Waiver Request/Remediation Plan workflow, which may loop back to the Credit Committee. [S053] If no breach is detected, continue with the standard monitoring cycle. [S054]

## 6. Exceptions & Escalations

Any deal that exceeds the Bank's Single Borrower Limit or Industry Concentration Limit necessitates additional approval from the Chief Risk Officer, irrespective of the assigned Risk Rating [S056].

If the independent appraisal cannot be completed within 15 business days due to site access issues, the deal timeline is placed on hold [S057]. The client is subsequently notified of the delay and provided with a revised expected decision date [S057].

In cases where a material adverse change in the borrower's financial position is identified after the Credit Memo has been submitted but prior to disbursement, the Underwriter is required to refresh the analysis [S058]. This refreshed analysis must then be re-submitted to the original approval authority before any funds are released [S058].

Furthermore, if the appraised Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio exceeds the Bank's policy maximum, typically 75%, the deal is flagged for escalation to Senior Risk Management to seek a policy exception [S022]. Similarly, if a confirmed sanctions or AML hit is identified (and is not a false positive), the deal is immediately escalated to the Financial Crime Unit, and all processing is suspended pending the outcome of their investigation [S024].

## 7. Service Level Agreements (SLA)

The following timelines define the expected service standards for various stages of the commercial loan underwriting and approval process.

Initial screening decisions must be made within 2 business days of Deal Screening Form submission [S060].
Parallel workstream completion, encompassing Financial Analysis, Valuation, and Compliance, is expected within 10 business days following screening approval [S061].
Credit Memo preparation is to be completed within 3 business days after all parallel workstreams have cleared [S062].
A Credit Committee decision should be rendered within 5 business days of Deal Package submission, provided it is considered at the next scheduled session, and no later than 10 business days from submission [S063].
Documentation and disbursement are to be finalized within 10 business days of loan approval, contingent upon the satisfaction of all Conditions Precedent [S064].
