## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for employees to request leave and for managers to approve or reject such requests within the HR Self-Service Portal [S003].

## 2. Scope

This SOP defines the standard process for employees to request leave and for managers to approve or reject such requests within the HR Self-Service Portal [S003]. This procedure applies to all full-time and part-time employees [S005] requesting annual leave, sick leave, or unpaid leave [S005].

## 3. Roles & Responsibilities

The Employee is responsible for initiating the leave request [S007]. The Reporting Manager's duty involves reviewing and then approving or rejecting the submitted request [S008]. Following the approval of a leave request, the HR Coordinator is accountable for updating the relevant leave balance records [S009].

## 4. Process Steps

The process for employee leave requests begins with the employee initiating the request [S010]. The employee first logs into the HR Self-Service Portal [S011]. Subsequently, the employee navigates to 'Leave Management' and selects 'New Leave Request' [S012]. Following this, the employee selects the applicable leave type (Annual, Sick, or Unpaid), along with the start date and end date for the leave period [S013]. A reason for the leave must be entered in the comments field [S014]. Once all details are entered, the employee clicks 'Submit Request' [S015]. Upon submission, the system automatically notifies the Reporting Manager via email [S016]. The Reporting Manager then reviews the request details and checks the available leave balance [S017] before clicking 'Approve' or 'Reject' [S018]. If the request is approved, the system updates the leave calendar and notifies the Employee [S019]. If the request is rejected, the Manager enters a rejection reason, and the system notifies the Employee with that specific reason [S020]. At month-end, the HR Coordinator verifies the updated leave balance in the HRMS [S021].

## 5. Decision Points

The process includes distinct decision points to ensure proper leave management. The first critical decision is whether the employee's available leave balance is sufficient for the requested period [S023]. Should the leave balance be insufficient, the system automatically blocks the submission of the request and provides an explicit error message to the Employee [S024]. Following the system's check and potential submission, the subsequent decision rests with the Reporting Manager [S025]. The Reporting Manager reviews the request details alongside the available leave balance [S017] and then makes a decision by clicking 'Approve' or 'Reject' [S018]. If the Reporting Manager approves the request, the system proceeds to update the leave calendar [S025]. Conversely, if the request is rejected, the manager must provide a reason for the rejection, and the system will notify the Employee with this specified reason, without updating the leave calendar [S025].

## 6. Exceptions

6. Exceptions [S026] If the Reporting Manager does not respond within 3 business days, the request is auto-escalated to the Department Head [S027].
